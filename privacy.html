Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Forest Seed Journey is designed as an offline, single-player game. This Privacy Policy explains how the game handles information when you use it.

Information We Collect

Forest Seed Journey does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information. The game does not require an account, sign-in, phone number, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone access, camera access, or any other personal information.

Game Data Stored on Your Device

The game may store game progress and sound preferences locally on your device so that your settings and completed levels can be remembered. This local data is not sent to us or to any third party by the game.

Tracking, Advertising, and Analytics

Forest Seed Journey does not use advertising, third-party analytics, cross-app tracking, or tracking technologies. It does not create advertising profiles or use your information for targeted advertising.

Children's Privacy

Because the game does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Services

The game itself does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, account, or payment services. Apple may process information as part of providing the App Store and iOS services under Apple's own policies; this is outside the game's direct collection practices.

Data Security

Because the game does not transmit personal information to us, there is no personal-information account or server database operated by us for the game.
